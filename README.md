# Broke Ballers data

Public NBA data for the Broke Ballers app: `nba.json` (players on current NBA rosters, per-game
stats, salaries, cities). The app downloads it to stay current between app versions.

Nothing about users is here or is ever sent here.

Stats and salaries: Basketball-Reference.com (via github.com/sumitrodatta/bball-reference-datasets).
Teams appear by city only. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the NBA or its teams.
